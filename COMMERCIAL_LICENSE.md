## When do you need a commercial license?

A commercial license is required if you:

- Use SkoluBoard as part of a **proprietary or closed-source product**
- Integrate SkoluBoard into a **paid service** offered to third parties
- Distribute SkoluBoard **without making your source code available**

## Who can use it for free?

Under GPL v3, you may use SkoluBoard for free if you:

- Are a **school, municipality, or non-profit organization**
- Use it for **internal, non-commercial purposes**
- Distribute your modifications under the **same GPL v3 license**
